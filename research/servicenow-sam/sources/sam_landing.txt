Software Asset Management
 Release version: Brazil

 1 minute to read
 The ServiceNow® 
 Software Asset Management (SAM) application systematically tracks, evaluates, and manages
 software licenses, compliance, and optimization. You can reclaim unused software rights,
 purchase new software rights, and manage allocations for entitlements.
 Watch this short video for an introduction to the Software Asset Management application.
 Note: Beginning with the ServiceNow AI Platform 
 Brazil release, limited support is provided for the Software Asset Management classic user interface. While it remains active in your instance, including when you upgrade to a new ServiceNow AI Platform release, you're encouraged to move to the new workspace experience.

Components installed with Software Asset Management Professional 
 Release version: Brazil 
 Updated September 10, 2026 
 5 minutes to read
 Roles, plugins, and scheduled jobs are installed with the activation of the Software Asset Management Professional application. Demo data is available with this application.
 Scheduled jobs installed
 To view the list of scheduled jobs installed with the Software Asset Management Professional application, navigate to your ServiceNow instance, select All , and enter Scheduled jobs in the application navigator. On the Scheduled jobs page, select the Show/hide filter and in the
 condition builder, enter the filter condition, Name starts with SAM .

Create success goals for Software Asset Management 
 Release version: Brazil 
 Updated September 10, 2026 
 1 minute to read
 Create success goals to track the success of the Software Asset Management 
 application in your instance.
 Before you begin
 Role required: sam_admin
 Procedure
 Navigate to Workspaces > Software Asset Workspace > Success Portal . 
 Select Create success goal . 
 On the form, fill in the details. 
 For a description of the field values, see SAM Success Goal Details .
 Select Save . 
 The success goal appears in the License operations view under the Success goals list. You can cancel or delete a success goal from the License operations view.

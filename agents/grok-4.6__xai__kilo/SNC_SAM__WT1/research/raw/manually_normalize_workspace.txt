Manually normalize a software model in the Software Asset Workspace 
 Release version: Brazil 
 Updated September 10, 2026 
 1 minute to read
 You can manually normalize a software discovery model that
 hasn’t been fully normalized
 (partially normalized, publisher normalized, or match not found)
 to reconcile it.
 Before you begin
 Role required: sam_admin or sam_user
 Procedure
 Navigate to All > Software asset > Software Asset Workspace > License operations > Discovery models . 
 Select a discovery model record that has a normalization status of
 Partially Normalized , Publisher Normalized , or Match Not Found . 
 Select the ellipsis icon on the right hand side of the page. 
 Select Normalize . 
 The normalization status changes to Manually Normalized .
